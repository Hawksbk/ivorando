Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Ivorando ("the app") is a chess opening trainer for Android, developed and published by the individual developer named on the app's Google Play page ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

The short version

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting.
  • Your progress, settings and lines stay on your phone. We do not receive them.
  • The app only goes online when you use an internet feature (tournaments or importing games), and then it talks directly to that chess service, not to us.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress, settings, avatar and name, coins, achievements, your own lines, imported games, exam results, your free-trial date and whether you bought Pro. This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not collect it, and it is never sent to us.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this data in your personal device backup to your Google account, under Google's terms. We cannot access these backups.

You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Internet features

The app connects to the internet only when you use these features:

  • Official tournaments. The app downloads public tournament broadcasts and games from Lichess (lichess.org, operated by Lichess, a French non-profit association).
  • Importing games. When you import games from Lichess, the app sends the username you type to Lichess to download that player's public games.

In both cases your device connects directly to Lichess. Like any website, it receives technical information such as your IP address and the app's name and version (which include our support email address so the service can contact us, not you). Its own privacy policy applies: https://lichess.org/privacy

We do not receive or store this information.

Purchases

Ivorando Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and handles your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that Pro was purchased, which it uses to unlock Pro on your device and to restore it after a reinstall. We never see your card or payment details.

Sharing and exporting

When you export or share lines or games, the app hands the text to the app you choose (for example email or a messenger). What happens next is up to you and that app.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is not designed specifically for children under 13. If you are a parent and have a question, contact us at the address below.

Your rights

Under privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU and UK) and the CCPA (California), you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal data. Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete: all app data stays on your device and is under your control. If you have a question about your data, write to us and we will answer.

Security

Connections to the chess services and Google Play use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change is significant, for example if the app starts collecting new information, we will point it out in the app or on its Google Play page before it takes effect.
